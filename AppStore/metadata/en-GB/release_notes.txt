What's New in DevSweep 1.4

Now in five languages. DevSweep speaks English, German, Japanese, French and Spanish. It follows your Mac's language, and a new Language button in the header lets you pick one yourself — the app relaunches, nothing is moved or deleted, and your last scan is kept. First launch starts with the language choice.

Room to grow. The header rearranges itself for longer languages and larger text sizes instead of cutting labels off.

Under the hood: every piece of text the app shows now comes from one String Catalog, counts use proper plurals, and sizes, numbers and dates follow your region's format.
